Corners are the four combinations the design has to hold up in: team size 3 or 5 (doc 01 — 3 is the minimum to queue) × gear ×1.00 (fresh) or ×1.12 (full BiS, the 12% Rule). Boss HP is derived per tier per team size at that tier's reference gear, so headcount never decides the outcome — a 3-stack and a 5-stack meet an equally hard boss. What separates the tiers is slack: gear is worth only ~11% of kill time, so a tier locks out an under-geared group only when its kill target sits inside that 11% of the enrage timer. Boss-damage and regen multipliers change how the fight feels and how much healing it demands, but they cannot gate anyone while no hero HP exists in canon and deaths therefore aren't simulated.

Player buffs & coverage — what the team's own attack and defensive cooldowns are worth once you account for how much of the fight they actually cover

Attack buffs are separate from Amp: doc 03 gives them as Power/damage buffs (Vygotsky Cultural Tools +15% Power, Bandura Self-Efficacy +10% per success to +50%, Seligman Signature Strengths), while Amp is the support/overheal term in the pipeline. Defensive coverage is the mitigation side (Freud Defense Mechanism, Piaget Schema Shield, Ainsworth Safe Haven, Maslow Safety Needs 12% max-HP shield, Pavlov's 20% DR inside his phase). Coverage is the honest half of the number: a 30% mitigation that is up half the fight is worth 15%.
